Field-level calibration

CaliDoc

My research · with Prof. Mohamed Cheriet

Problem
A model can be confident about each word but still return a wrong total or date. Downstream systems use whole fields, so that is what needs a reliability check.
My part
I designed the method, ran the experiments, and wrote the code, the paper and the demo.
Built
A calibration layer that sends each extracted field to pass, review or abstain, plus an audit that flags likely annotation errors. It is evaluated on four receipt and form datasets.

Multi-agent orchestration

InvestmentIQ

Team capstone, Agentic AI course · 3 people · 2025

Problem
Investment signals come from financial statements, analyst ratings and news, and they often disagree. We wanted one system that weighs them and explains its recommendation.
My part
I was lead architect. I designed the multi-agent structure, built the LangGraph workflow and orchestration, and connected it to the Streamlit dashboard. I also merged my teammates’ agents into the main branch.
Built
Five specialist agents feed a signal-fusion step. When their signals conflict, a debate step runs before the recommendation. In the released code, the financial and market agents use live market data, and the other three use sample data by default.

Team credit: Murthy Vanapalli built the market-data integration, price targets and recommendation thresholds. Ameya built the qualitative-analysis agent.

Grounded answers over PDFs

Auralynq-RAG

Independent open source · 2026

Problem
Most document chat tools give you an answer and a file name. You still have to find the supporting passage yourself.
My part
I built it on my own: retrieval, the agentic answer loop, the grounding layer, the voice input and the web interface.
Built
A local-first system you can also query by voice. Each citation in an answer opens the PDF page with the supporting region outlined. If a passage has no stored coordinates, the system shows the whole page and labels the citation as page-level.

The Hugging Face demo is currently paused. The repository runs locally without a GPU or API keys.

Document layout

FasterHAND

My research · with A. Rahiche and M. Cheriet

Problem
Handwritten pages mix body text, marginal notes and several columns. Most pipelines segment the page first, and segmentation errors carry into the transcription.
My part
I wrote the model, training and evaluation code, trained the released weights, and built the demo.
Built
A model that reads a page as one sequence of characters and layout tokens in reading order. It is compared with the public DAN weights on READ 2016 under the same protocol, on one, two and three pages at a time.

Handwriting recognition

HTR-JAND

My research · with A. Rahiche and M. Cheriet

Problem
Accurate line recognizers are often too large to run cheaply on big archives.
My part
I designed the architecture and the distillation setup, ran the experiments, and released the code and models.
Built
A compact line recognizer that a larger teacher model trains. It is evaluated on IAM, RIMES, Bentham, Washington and Saint Gall.

More projects, team work and competitions

Research · journal paper

SAGE

A framework for evaluating LLM agents the way they behave in deployment: stability over long runs, operating cost, exposure at tool and protocol boundaries, and the effect of corrective action. I wrote the reference implementation that accompanies the IEEE TAI paper.

Research · preprint

MARL-IoTP

Multi-agent reinforcement learning in which IoT devices choose perception models and edge servers allocate compute, with a learned message channel between them. I am the first author and wrote the simulation and training code.

Research · under review

SCALE

Measures when sparse attention between agents actually reduces latency on GPUs, and counts the cost of choosing which peers to keep. The repository holds the code, kernels and measurement records for a manuscript under review.

Research · conference paper

Curriculum learning for documents

Tests whether a progressive data schedule shortens training for BERT and LayoutLMv3 across six document datasets. Matched-compute baselines and ablations show where the saving comes from. Published at ICAART 2026.

Teaching · independent

ROSE Explorer

Eleven notebooks that explain and check in code every equation of a survey of generative models, plus an interactive app with seven demos covering sampling, decoding and evaluation.

Independent

VectorSphere

Team challenge entry · 5 people · 2026

SHL Challenge 2026: FeatureFlyers

Recognizes eight transport and locomotion modes from phone sensor data, with frozen foundation models as the challenge requires. I maintained the team repository and built the staged pipeline that ends in the team’s final LightGBM ensemble. The team’s primary contact was Ahmed Bali.

Team project · 2022

Deep Tsundoku

Finds and recommends books from a photo of a bookshelf. I contributed the first baseline recommenders, built on TF-IDF and Word2Vec, and a Donut fine-tuning run. Teammates built the deployed spine reader, the book-cover model and the final recommender.

About me.

I’m a data scientist and machine learning engineer based in Montréal. I work at SITA and did my doctoral research at École de technologie supérieure.

At the Synchromédia Laboratory, I studied how a model can read an entire handwritten page, including its margins and reading order. My work now also asks when a model’s output is reliable enough to use and when a person should review it.

Outside my own research, I build open-source tools for grounded document question answering. I have also worked on team projects and challenge entries in agentic AI and sensor-based recognition. The project list above says which parts were mine.
